About

I am a full-stack developer at Fordham University. My daily work spans both front-end and back-end development, as well as web content platform engineering and firewall security services. I am actively involved in building and updating application services for the university to implement and utilize, including optimized platforms for students and faculty such as the new Fordham Mobile App, Electronic ID, and the Final Exam Scheduler.
While I predominantly focus on application services and development, I am also heavily involved in our efforts for redeveloping and implementing an updated and user-friendly search function on our website, troubleshooting structured content within our CMS, analyzing and remediating threats through WAF rule sets on Cloudflare, and being a point person for a variety of our vendors and associates.

I graduated from Fordham University in May 2022 with a Bachelor of Arts, double-majoring in Computer Science and Economics, with double minors in French and History. Shortly there after, I completed a Master of Science in Computer Science at Fordham in May 2023. My capstone project was a semi-linear scavenger game developed for incoming students to familiarize themselves with the Fordham Rose Hill campus. The game was built in Python, utilizing the PyGame library and completely original assets.

I am interested in full-time software developer and engineering roles across sectors, including video games, information technology, and financial markets.
